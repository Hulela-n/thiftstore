WHY DO WE EXIST?

We exist bridge the gap in fast fashion clothing supply across South Africa's nine provinces. Currently fast fashion clothing is in the hands of established retailers that do not supply all the fast fashion in at the level of international retails. Consumers were naturally drawn to these international retailers but with the introduction of tarrifs on those goods, South Africans became averse to this option. With our 'unique positioning' and deep market research we are able to align oursleves and source this everchanging fast fashion, without added tarrifs for our services.
